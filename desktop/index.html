Keystroke mode—
Orion—
Hey Orion — Say “Hey Orion”, wait for the chime, say what you want, then press OK on the phone. When Orion asks a question, say “yes” or “no”. Heard offline: nothing leaves the laptop until you press OK.
Dictation—
Remembers you — Orion's long-term memory of you on Backboard: people, preferences, routines, and what you asked before. Say “remember that…”, or just mention things.
Screen memory — Keeps a screenshot whenever the screen changes and Gemini captions it, so you can ask Orion later: “what was that error earlier?”, “reopen the page I was reading”. Screenshots go to Google (Gemini); password, bank and chat windows are skipped.
Gap between keys 0 ms Spaces out fast repeats ("right right right"). ~300 ms for games that animate each move, like Bloxorz.
Pointer—
Doing — The phone's Menu button opens the wheel around the pointer: Zoom, Click types, Scroll, More (Orion, Keys, Pointer settings, Pause). Point at a tile and press Left.
Dwell click 800 ms now 800 ms Hold still on something to select it. The time adapts: a little shorter after selections that go well, longer after a quick retry.
Snap to buttons The pointer jumps onto the nearest button, link or field. Apps must have accessibility on (see the README).
AI targets Gemini looks at the screen and finds the buttons that the accessibility tree misses. It looks again only when the screen changes, a few cents each time.
Drift correction 0, 0 px Learned from each selection on a small button. Center resets it.
